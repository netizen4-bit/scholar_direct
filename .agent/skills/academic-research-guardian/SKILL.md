## PILLAR 1: TURNITIN-PROOF ORGANIC ACADEMIC PROSE (<15% AI DETECTION)

AI detectors (Turnitin, GPTZero, CopyLeaks) flag text based on **Perplexity** (predictability of word choice) and **Burstiness** (variation in sentence length and syntax rhythm). To guarantee an organic, human score (<15%):

### 1.1 Burstiness & Sentence Length Asymmetry
- **The Golden Rule:** Never write consecutive sentences of similar length (18–22 words).
- **Asymmetric Coupling:** Pair a dense, compound 30–35 word sentence (utilizing semicolons, qualifying clauses, or dependent prepositions) immediately with a crisp, punchy 7–10 word sentence.
- **Predicative Variation (Turkish):** Avoid monotonous endings in "...-maktadır / ...-mektedir". Blend multiple tense and aspect markers: "...-r", "...-mıştır", "...gözlenmektedir", "...ortaya koymaktadır", "...söylenebilir".

### 1.2 Blacklist of Banned AI Clichés & Natural Alternatives

| Category | ❌ Banned AI Cliché (Do NOT Use) | ✅ Approved Academic Alternative (Use Instead) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **English (Intro/Transitions)** | *Delve into, tapestry, testament, underscore, crucial, pivotal, paramount, navigate the landscape* | *Examines, evaluates, encapsulates, suggests, fundamental, substantive, contextualize* |
| **English (Connectors)** | *Moreover, furthermore (in consecutive lines), in conclusion, it is important to note that, shed light on* | *Yet, still, empirically, by contrast, from a methodological standpoint, the evidence indicates* |
| **Turkish (Kalıplar)** | *Buna karşın (her paragraf başında), özetle, sonuç olarak, kapsamlı bir şekilde, önemli bir rol oynamaktadır* | *Ne var ki, fakat, öte yandan, bununla birlikte, ilgili literatür incelendiğinde, pratikte ise* |
| **Turkish (Boş Sözler)** | *Altını çizmek gerekir ki, günümüz dünyasında, kritik bir öneme sahiptir, yadsınamaz bir gerçektir ki* | *Mevcut bulgular ışığında, bu doğrultuda, yöntemsel açıdan, ampirik göstergeler* |
| **Dual-Lingual Syntax** | *Not only... but also... / Sadece X değil, aynı zamanda Y* | Rephrase into distinct qualifying clauses with active verbs. |

### 1.3 Domain-Specific Terminology Lock (No Layman Translations)
Preserve formal technical terms in their native disciplinary context:
- **Econometrics:** *OLS (EKK), Fixed Effects (Sabit Etkiler), Random Effects (Tesadüfi Etkiler), Endogeneity (İçsellik), Cointegration (Eşbütünleşme), Instrumental Variables (Araç Değişkenler), Multicollinearity (Çoklu Doğrusal Bağlantı).*
- **Finance & FinTech:** *Financial Inclusion (Finansal Kapsayıcılık — NOT "dahil edilme"), Default Risk (Temerrüt Riski), Credit Scoring (Kredi Skorlama), Asset Pricing (Varlık Fiyatlama).*
- **Decision Sciences:** *AHP Consistency Ratio (CR < 0.10 Tutarlılık Oranı), Relative Closeness (Göreceli Yakınlık Katsayısı CC_i), Kendall's W Concordance (Uyum Katsayısı).*
- **Data Science & MIS:** *Explainable AI (XAI / Açıklanabilir Yapay Zeka), Algorithmic Fairness (Algoritmik Adalet), Technology Acceptance Model (Teknoloji Kabul Modeli TAM).*

### 1.4 Anti-Translationese (Türkçe Çeviri Kokusu Temizliği)
- **Calque Inversion:** English passive-participle constructions ("By analyzing the data, we observed...") must not be translated literally as "Verileri analiz ederek gözlemledik...". Instead, write: "Elde edilen ampirik veriler modellendiğinde, değişkenler arasındaki yapısal ilişkinin...".
- **Case Agreement (İsmin Halleri):** Check dative/locative/ablative case endings across conjoined phrases (e.g., "...modellerine ve parametrelerine dayanarak" instead of "...modelleriyle ve parametrelere").

## PILLAR 2: ZERO-SYNTHETIC REAL METHODOLOGY ENFORCEMENT

All research proposals, empirical models, and equations MUST originate from the verified `ESTABLISHED_METHODOLOGIES` whitelist. Synthetic, AI-hallucinated methods (e.g., "quantum neural regression" or ungrounded hybrid algorithms) are strictly prohibited.

### 2.1 Whitelisted Methodological Families & Diagnostic Batteries

#### 1. Econometrics & Regression
- **Estimators:** OLS with White/HC1-HC3 robust SE, Panel Fixed Effects (Within), Panel Random Effects (GLS), System GMM (Blundell-Bond), Difference-in-Differences (DiD), ARDL.
- **Mandatory Diagnostics:**
  - Multicollinearity: Variance Inflation Factor ($VIF < 5.0$).
  - Heteroskedasticity: Breusch-Pagan or White test ($p > 0.05$ or robust standard errors mandatory).
  - Autocorrelation: Durbin-Watson statistic ($1.50 - 2.50$) or Breusch-Godfrey test.
  - Specification Gate: Hausman Test ($p < 0.05$ mandates Fixed Effects over Random Effects).
  - Residual Normality: Jarque-Bera test.

#### 2. Survival & Duration Analysis
- **Estimators:** Semi-parametric Cox Proportional Hazards Model, Kaplan-Meier Product-Limit Estimator.
- **Mandatory Diagnostics:**
  - Proportional Hazards Assumption: Schoenfeld residuals global and covariate test ($p > 0.05$).
  - Discrimination: Harrell's Concordance Index ($C\text{-index} > 0.70$).

#### 3. Multi-Criteria Decision Making (MCDM / MCDA)
- **Estimators:** Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP), TOPSIS (Technique for Order Preference by Similarity to Ideal Solution), VIKOR, DEMATEL, Best-Worst Method (BWM).
- **Mandatory Diagnostics:**
  - Saaty Consistency Ratio ($CR = \frac{CI}{RI} < 0.10$ Hard Gate). If $CR \ge 0.10$, the pairwise matrix is invalid.
  - Sensitivity Analysis: Systematic weight perturbation ($\pm 20\%$) across criterion rankings.

#### 4. Machine Learning & Explainable AI (XAI)
- **Estimators:** Random Forest, Extra-Trees, Gradient Boosting (XGBoost), SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations).
- **Mandatory Diagnostics:**
  - Performance: Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation, ROC-AUC ($> 0.75$), Balanced F1-Score.
  - Algorithmic Fairness: Disparate Impact Ratio within $[0.80, 1.25]$.

#### 5. Iterative Consensus & Systems Thinking
- **Estimators:** Classical/Modified Delphi Method, Causal Loop Diagramming (CLD).
- **Mandatory Diagnostics:**
  - Panel Consensus: Kendall's $W$ concordance coefficient ($p < 0.05$ rejects null of divergence).
  - Interquartile Range ($IQR \le 1.0$ on 5-point Likert).
  - Feedback Loop Polarity: Reinforcing ($R$) vs. Balancing ($B$) feedback loop identification.
